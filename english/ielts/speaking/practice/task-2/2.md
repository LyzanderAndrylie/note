### Identified Issues & Examiner Notes

1. **Past Tense Consistency in Storytelling**:
   - _Original_: _"So what's happening was we arrived..."_ / _"Of course it is not free. We need to pay..."_ / _"we just negotiate..."_
   - _Fix_: Keep the narrative frame firmly in the past: _"What happened was..."_, _"It wasn't free; we had to pay..."_, _"we negotiated the price..."_
2. **Connotation & Collocation Precision**:
   - _Original_: _"it was just an accident actually"_
   - _Issue_: _"Accident"_ usually implies an unfortunate mistake or mishap.
   - _Fix_: Use ==spontaneous==, ==unplanned==, or ==pure serendipity / coincidence==.
   - _Original_: _"take that moment"_ $\rightarrow$ _Fix_: ==capture that moment / freeze that memory in time==.
3. **Quantifiers & Plural Forms**:
   - _Original_: _"how many time it took"_ $\rightarrow$ _Fix_: ==how much time it would take / how long it would take== (_time_ is uncountable when referring to duration).
   - _Original_: _"one of the prestigious university"_ $\rightarrow$ _Fix_: ==one of the most prestigious universities== (_"one of the + plural noun"_).
4. **Prepositions & Phrasal Idioms**:
   - _Original_: _"at my graduation day"_ $\rightarrow$ _Fix_: ==on my graduation day== (use _on_ for specific days).
   - _Original_: _"came across to our sight"_ $\rightarrow$ _Fix_: ==crossed our path== or ==happened to pass by==.
5. **Spoken Discourse & Filler Clean-up**:
   - _Original_: _"So yeah, simply we just negotiate the price, and for example like... and yeah something like that."_
   - _Tip_: Avoid ending spoken points with vague fillers like _"and yeah something like that"_. Instead, use concise concluding phrases such as _"and things of that nature"_ or transition cleanly to the next point.

## 2. Band 8.5–9.0 Strategy & Model Answer

### Strategy & Coaching Tips for Part 2

- **The Narrative Arc (Storytelling Framework)**:
  1. **Hook & Context (15–20s)**: Introduce the photograph with emotional weight rather than a robotic formula (_"I'd like to talk about..."_).
  2. **The Backstory & Setting (30–40s)**: Paint the atmosphere (the bustle of graduation morning, arriving early, the grandeur of the campus).
  3. **The Serendipitous Incident (30–40s)**: Narrate the unexpected interaction with the roving photographer with vivid details (negotiating the shot, picking the backdrop).
  4. **The Visual Details & Emotional Resonance (30–40s)**: Describe the photo's composition, the expressions, and why it remains a cherished heirloom in your household.
- **Upgrading Discourse Markers**: Replace casual fillers (_"so yeah"_, _"like"_) with sophisticated signposts: _==As fate would have it==_, _==What makes this picture particularly memorable is==_, _==Looking back at it now==_.
