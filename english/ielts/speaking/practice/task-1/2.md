## Key Identified Issues & Spoken Grammar / Lexical Notes

1. **Unnatural Collocations for Clothing & Wear**:
   - _Incorrect_: `"I don't know how it feels using them."`
   - _Fix_: `"I don't really know what it feels like to wear them."`
   - _Explanation_: In English, we **wear** or **put on** clothes, we do not _use_ them.

2. **Double Past Time Modifiers**:
   - _Incorrect_: `"I've just recently graduated from Universitas Indonesia last year."`
   - _Fix_: `"I graduated from Universitas Indonesia last year"` OR `"I recently graduated from..."`
   - _Explanation_: Combining _"just recently"_ (present perfect / immediate past) with _"last year"_ (specific completed past time) is grammatically conflicting and redundant.

3. **Adjective vs. Adverb in Compound Noun Phrases**:
   - _Incorrect_: `"That's my usually go to for work."`
   - _Fix_: `"That's usually my go-to for work."` OR `"That's my usual go-to outfit for work."`
   - _Explanation_: _"Go-to"_ acts as a noun/adjective phrase; _"usually"_ is an adverb that should modify the verb (_"is usually"_), whereas _"usual"_ is the adjective.

4. **Singular/Plural & Pronoun Reference Consistency**:
   - _Incorrect_: `"I personally use maps on my smartphone... In addition, I also use it..."`
   - _Fix_: `"...use navigation apps on my smartphone... I also rely on them..."`
   - _Explanation_: If you introduce _"maps"_ in the plural, refer back using _"them"_, not _"it"_. Alternatively, refer to _"the map app"_ (singular).

5. **Spoken Discourse Markers vs. Written Connectors**:
   - _Issue_: Overusing academic/essay connectors like `"In addition"` and redundant hedging like `"From my perspective, I think..."`.
   - _Fix_: In IELTS Speaking Part 1, prefer natural spoken markers: _"To be honest,"_, _"Besides,"_, _"I'd say,"_, _"Actually,"_, _"In my view,"_.

6. **Slip/Misstatement**:
   - _Original_: `"Unfortunately, I don't hate maps at home..."`
   - _Intended_: `"I don't have maps at home..."`
   - _Note_: Ensure clear enunciation between _have_ /hæv/ and _hate_ /heɪt/.

## Part 1 Coaching Strategy: The A-R-E-A Method

To consistently achieve Band 8.0–9.0 in Speaking Part 1, structure each answer using **2 to 4 natural spoken sentences**:

```
[A] Answer directly    → State your preference or answer clearly in natural spoken English.
[R] Reason             → Explain the primary underlying reason or motive.
[E] Example / Detail   → Give a concrete personal instance, habit, or illustration.
[A] Alternative / Past → (Optional) Contrast with what you don't do, or past vs. present.
```
