## IELTS Mock 2026 January

1. Describe a bad service you received in a restaurant/shop
   a. You should say:
   i. when and where it happened
   ii. what happened
   iii. how you reacted
   iv. And explain how you felt about the experience

= Back when I was celebrating my friend’s birthday, we went to gyukaku, an all you can eat restaurant, at Mall of Indonesia. It was a great place to held a birthday party, as the food was actually quite delicious. ==To give you an idea, because it was our first time cooking the food ourselves, it went terribly wrong. Specifically, the fat from the meat dripped on the grill causing a massive flare up. Our first instinct was to pour water on the grill. Fortunately, the staff immediately rushed over to extinguish the fire. However, because of this, they spoke to us quite harshly.== I mean as the first timer, we did not understand and a bit shock of the situation. I wish they could be more gentle to us. Overall, while the food was great, the experience of celebrating my friend’s birthday was diminished.
