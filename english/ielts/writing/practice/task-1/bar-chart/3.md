### Note

- **Past Participle vs. Simple Past (`shown` vs. `showed` / `exhibited`):**
  - _"Shown"_ is a past participle and requires an auxiliary verb (e.g., _"has shown"_). For historical events/trends in Task 1, use the simple past tense (_"showed"_, _"exhibited"_, _"displayed"_).
- **Correlative Preposition Pairs (`between ... and ...` vs. `from ... to ...`):**
  - Never pair _"between"_ with _"to"_. Use either **"between 1960 and 2000"** or **"from 1960 to 2000"**.
- **Article & Plural Noun Agreement:**
  - _"a steady increments"_ $\rightarrow$ Indefinite article _"a"_ cannot modify plural _"increments"_. Correct: **"in steady increments"** or **"at a steady increment"**.
  - _"a significant proportions"_ $\rightarrow$ Correct: **"a significant proportion"** (singular) or **"significant proportions"** (without _"a"_).
- **Participial Clause (`contribute` $\rightarrow$ `contributing`):**
  - In absolute and prepositional phrases with _"with + noun + participle"_, use the present participle: _"with the other two sectors **contributing** at around 15%"_.
- **Possessive Apostrophe & Pronoun Agreement:**
  - _"those two sectors contributions"_ $\rightarrow$ Needs possessive: **"those two sectors' contributions"**.
  - _"from its previous level"_ $\rightarrow$ Because the subject refers to two sectors (plural), use **"their previous levels"**.
- **Percentage vs. Percentage Points:**
  - When comparing differences between two percentage figures (e.g., 63% to 14%), describe the change as **percentage points** (a drop of 49 percentage points) or describe the overall values directly, rather than stating "a 40% decrease" (which mathematically means $63\% \times (1 - 0.40) \approx 37.8\%$).
- **Discourse Marker Appropriacy (`Alternatively`):**
  - _"Alternatively"_ introduces a substitute choice or alternative option. When reporting concurrent, background factual trends, use **"Meanwhile"**, **"In contrast"**, or **"By comparison"**.
- **Academic Collocations:**
  - Avoid _"took a significant proportion"_; prefer standard academic verbs: **"accounted for"**, **"represented"**, **"comprised"**, or **"constituted"**.
  - Avoid clumsy phrases like _"jumped at around two fold"_; use **"roughly doubled"** or **"increased twofold"**.
