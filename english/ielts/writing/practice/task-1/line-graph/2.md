### Detailed Criterion Breakdown

#### Task Achievement (Band 6.0)

- **Strengths**: Accurately recognizes that the prompt deals with historical data (2010–2020) and future projections (2020–2040). Identifies the general ascent of the Internet and decline of traditional print media.
- **Areas for Improvement**:
  - **Omission of Key Initial Data**: The starting values in 2010 (TV at ~36%, Newspapers at ~34%, Magazines at 15%, Radio at 10%, Internet at ~3%) are barely presented.
  - **Inaccurate Data Representation**: 47% is described as "just above 45%" and called "the majority" (a majority strictly means >50%; below 50% is a "plurality" or "largest single share"). Television in 2040 finishes around 37%, not 35%.
  - **Speculation / Subjective Language**: Avoid explaining _why_ allocations change (_"by putting more fund into it"_ or _"prioritized"_); Task 1 requires an objective transfer of factual visual data only.

#### Coherence & Cohesion (Band 6.0)

- **Strengths**: Logical paragraph division (Introduction, Overview, 2010–2020 Historic Period, 2020–2040 Projections).
- **Areas for Improvement**:
  - **Awkward Discourse Markers**: _"Looking back between 2010 and 2020"_ sounds like personal memoir/reflection rather than an academic report. Use neutral temporal signposts such as _"Between 2010 and 2020"_ or _"During the first decade"_.
  - **Redundancy**: _"in the next subsequent years"_ is tautological (choose either _"subsequent years"_ or _"the following years"_).
  - **Faulty Referencing**: In paragraph 2, _"the others were just under 15%"_ leaves the antecedent ambiguous (others what? media types? proportions?).

#### Lexical Resource (Band 6.0)

- **Strengths**: Good attempt to vary terminology (_proportions, deprioritized, percentage points_).
- **Areas for Improvement**:
  - **Unnatural/Inappropriate Word Choices**: _"increment"_ and _"decrement"_ are technical/programming terms rather than standard descriptors for trends. Use _"increase/growth/surge"_ and _"decrease/decline/fall"_.
  - **Collocation Errors**: _"advertisement budget allocation plan"_ (it is advertising expenditure data, not an organization's plan); _"putting more fund"_ (unidiomatic; should be _"allocating more funds/capital"_).
  - **Misuse of "Majority"**: _"television and newspapers were the majority forms"_ (they were the _predominant_ or _leading_ mediums; "majority" means >50% of a single entity).

#### Grammatical Range & Accuracy (Band 5.5)

- **Strengths**: Attempts complex sentences using subordinate clauses, conjunctions (_while, thus_), and future tense markers.
- **Areas for Improvement**:
  - **Subject-Verb Agreement**: _"proportions of money was spent"_ $\rightarrow$ _"proportions... were spent"_ or _"a significant proportion... was spent"_.
  - **Pronoun Agreement**: _"...only television and internet saw an increasing trend... from its initial level"_ $\rightarrow$ _"their initial levels"_.
  - **Article & Countability Errors**: _"more fund"_ $\rightarrow$ _"more funds / funding"_; _"Internet"_ $\rightarrow$ _"the Internet"_; _"newspaper will be deprioritized"_ $\rightarrow$ _"newspapers will be..."_.
  - **Broken Conjunction / Omitted Subject**: _"...while at the same time will cause decrement to others"_ lacks a subject before the verb _"will cause"_.

### Note Section: Summary of Issues & Corrections

- **Issue 1 (GRA - Subject-Verb Agreement)**: _"significant proportions of money was spent"_
  - **Correction**: _"a significant proportion of money was spent"_ OR _"significant proportions of money were spent"_.
  - **Reason**: The plural subject "proportions" requires a plural verb "were", or singular "proportion" with "was".
  - \*\*Note\*\*\*:\*\* ==In IELTS Academic writing, native phrasing treats the combined or overall share as a singular fraction==.
- **Issue 2 (LR - Inappropriate Lexis / Jargon)**: _"increment"_ and _"decrement"_
  - **Correction**: _"increase / rise / growth"_ and _"decrease / decline / contraction"_.
  - **Reason**: "Increment/decrement" are step-based mathematical/computing terms, unnatural in descriptive trend analysis.
- **Issue 3 (LR - Collocation & Countability)**: _"putting more fund into it"_
  - **Correction**: _"allocating more funding / investing more capital"_.
  - **Reason**: "Fund" is countable (a fund / funds), and "putting fund" is colloquial.
- **Issue 4 (CC - Tautology / Redundancy)**: _"the next subsequent years"_
  - **Correction**: _"subsequent years"_ OR _"the following years"_.
  - **Reason**: "Next" and "subsequent" convey identical meanings.
- **Issue 5 (CC - Informal Discourse Marker)**: _"Looking back between 2010 and 2020"_
  - **Correction**: _"Between 2010 and 2020"_ OR _"Over the 2010–2020 period"_.
  - **Reason**: "Looking back" creates an informal, conversational tone unsuitable for an objective academic report.
- **Issue 6 (LR / TA - Inaccurate Use of 'Majority')**: _"majority forms of advertisement"_ / _"Internet will take the majority of budget at just above 45%"_
  - **Correction**: _"predominant forms"_ / _"capture the largest single share (plurality)"_.
  - **Reason**: "Majority" strictly denotes greater than 50%. An item with 45% or 47% is the _leading_ or _largest share_, not a majority.
- **Issue 7 (GRA - Missing Article & Capitalization)**: _"internet"_, _"Television"_, _"newspaper"_
  - **Correction**: _"the internet"_, lowercase _"television"_, plural _"newspapers"_.
  - **Reason**: "The internet" takes the definite article; media types should follow consistent lowercase styling and plural forms when generalizing.
- **Issue 8 (TA - Unhedged Future Predictions)**: _"Internet will take..."_, _"newspaper will be..."_
  - **Correction**: _"the internet is projected/forecasted/expected to reach..."_.
  - **Reason**: ==Future projections in IELTS Task 1 should be expressed with hedging language (_is predicted to, is expected to, is forecasted to_) rather than definitive future assertions (_will_)==.
