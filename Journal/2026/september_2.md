## LinkedIn Post: AACL-IJCNLP 2026

Excited to share that our paper "Sparse Autoencoders Can Capture Language-Specific Concepts Across Diverse Languages" has been accepted to AACL-IJCNLP 2026.

In this paper, we propose a method called SAE-LAPE to identify language-specific features in sparse autoencoders (SAEs) for Llama 3.2 1B and Gemma 2 (2B and 9B). Focusing on SAEs trained on each model's feed-forward network outputs across all layers, we find that:

1. Language-specific features exist and are interpretable.
2. These features influence both the perplexity and language output of LLMs.
3. They can be used for language classification, performing comparably to fastText while offering more interpretability.

Overall, this study offers new insights into the internal representations and multilingual mechanisms of LLMs through language-specific features from SAEs.

Paper: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11230>
