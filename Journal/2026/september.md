# Scribing - September 2026

It has been three months since I last wrote in my journal. Technically, I intend to write on a monthly basis. However, the decision is still largely determined by my own mood whether to write or not. As always, although I know consistency is the key to my own growth and personal goals, I am still a bit reluctant to spend my spare time doing something like writing. But, here I am, sitting and writing by myself to plan for my future. I hope this opportunity on a random day in September 2026 will help me achieve everything I have always wanted in my life. Maybe it sounds quite ridiculous, but I have always believed that spending my time thinking will help me in the long run. As I learned from the book by Haemin Sunim titled "The Things You Can See Only When You Slow Down", taking my time in this busy world is extremely important to obtain a crystal-clear path toward my own future. Of course, there are a lot of questions for which I still do not know the right answers; at least I am taking the time to reflect on them. So with this opportunity, I will try to list everything I want to cut out and the things I pray to God will happen for me in the future.

## Things to Be Cut

1. **Procrastination**: the habit of delaying and putting off various tasks and responsibilities.
   - Now that I am no longer a student, this tendency has become quite subtle. At work, my primary responsibility is simply to perform my duties as best as I can. However, I also have personal responsibilities toward my own growth, striving to become the best version of myself. With the freedom I now have, nobody is constantly directing me, and my parents often suggest I simply take things easy. As a result, I sometimes find myself putting off tasks that I know are fundamentally important for my future. I recognize that this is counterproductive, which is why I want to intentionally structure my time, clarify my life goals, and establish what I need to do on a daily basis.

2. **Fear of leaving my comfort zone**: hesitating to take on unfamiliar challenges and step into the unknown.
   - For most of us, venturing into the unknown where outcomes are uncertain can be intimidating. Once we achieve a certain level of stability, the temptation is often to simply play it safe. However, I have come to realize that decisive action is essential. _If not now, when? If not me, who?_ These two questions serve as a constant reminder of why I must continually push beyond my comfort zone.

3. **Fear of failure**: letting self-doubt and apprehension of falling short hold me back.
   - It is human nature to fear failure. Yet, as many who reflect on a long life often attest, one of their deepest regrets is not having the courage to risk failure in the first place. Failure is an integral part of the process that builds resilience, and it is often a necessary stepping stone toward achieving greater things.

4. **Poor energy management**: particularly suboptimal sleep habits and irregular sleep routines.
   - This is an area I have struggled with for as long as I can remember, throughout high school, university, and even now. Maintaining a consistent sleep schedule and healthy bedtime habits has always been difficult for me. While I know I would be much more productive if I went to bed early, I often stay up late trying to carve out time for activities that are hard to pursue during normal hours. As a consequence, I end up operating suboptimally throughout the day. This pattern has persisted for far too long, and I am determined to change it fundamentally.

5. **Unhealthy dietary habits**: neglecting nutritious, wholesome eating patterns.
   - While I understand the importance of eating nutritious meals, budget constraints and the convenience of relying on whatever is readily prepared often lead me to neglect my nutrition entirely. Moving forward, I need to be far more deliberate about nourishing my body properly.

6. **Lack of consistent exercise**: failing to maintain regular physical activity and workout habits.
   - The core dilemma here is how to manage my time wisely so that I can exercise consistently. At times, I feel I barely have enough hours for my highest priority, which is studying, leaving me wondering how to spare time for physical workouts. Currently, I only do basic exercises as part of Hal Elrod's SAVERS routine and walk home from work as my daily commute. Going forward, I need to establish a dedicated, sustainable exercise routine to safeguard my long-term health and vitality.

7. **Complacency**: taking problems too lightly and underestimating serious matters.
   - It is easy for anyone, myself included, to quickly forget past hardships and retreat into the safety of a comfort zone. When that happens, we settle into feeling that we have done enough, forgetting that true growth requires embracing discomfort and tackling difficult challenges. It is vital to remember that to become the best version of yourself, you must actively live the lifestyle that your ideal self would live.

8. **An attitude of apathy and indifference**: falling into a dismissive "bodo amat" (couldn't care less) mindset.
   - At times, I catch myself adopting a dismissive attitude toward how others feel, justifying it with the belief that I should only prioritize myself. While self-preservation has its place, falling into apathy and cynicism ultimately limits my character. Cultivating genuine empathy and moving away from a detached, indifferent mindset will undoubtedly serve me much better in the future.

9. **Aloofness and lack of care**: being detached and insufficiently attentive to my surroundings and the people around me.
   - I often catch myself thinking that people rarely change, asking myself, _“Who am I to change anyone?”_ For most of my life, I believed that only God, time, and hardship could truly transform a person. While that remains largely true, it is no excuse for emotional detachment. I must take responsibility for being supportive, cultivating greater empathy, and becoming more socially engaged and attentive to the people around me.

10. **Stress and burnout during setbacks**: succumbing to overwhelming stress and burnout whenever circumstances turn unfavorable or fail to meet expectations.
    - It is often said that burnout is deeply tied to one’s identity, personality, and desire for achievement. This certainly resonates with me; I feel burnout most acutely when I fall short of my own high expectations. While positive stress can be a natural byproduct of ambition and progress, letting it escalate into debilitating burnout is completely counterproductive. I need to learn to manage pressure with greater wisdom and emotional balance.

## Things I Pray to God

For me, the most logical avenue to create real impact is through artificial intelligence and computer science, even though I know the road ahead is challenging. Taking the IELTS was daunting, but I was grateful to surpass my target with an overall score of 7.5. Gaining admission to top global universities will require tremendous preparation, so I want to build solid daily habits, seize every opportunity to learn, and pave the way to study and work abroad. If I am to make an impact in Indonesia, I want to do so with wisdom and integrity, avoiding the kind of unjust political entanglements that have seen figures wrongfully targeted or imprisoned. I never want to find myself in a situation where immense sacrifice is met with betrayal, reminiscent of the classic Spider-Man line: _“You know how much I sacrificed?!”_ I want my contributions to be both deeply impactful and safeguarded.

2. **Wisdom and discernment**: wisdom to navigate every situation and life challenge, endowed with the divine wisdom and understanding of Solomon.
   - The story of King Solomon has always fascinated me. With no tangible evidence, he possessed the insight to discern which mother was lying and which was telling the truth. I pray to God for that same caliber of wisdom and discernment in my own life: the ability to see through complexity, make righteous judgments, and use that understanding to bless myself, my family, my community, and my nation.

I want to hone my coding proficiency and research skills so that I can build meaningful technologies and bring innovative ideas to life. To make a genuine impact, one must take action, and taking effective action demands deep technical competence. 4. **Continuous learning capacity**: the ability to always learn effectively, grasp complex subjects, and grow continuously.

5. **Sound decision-making**: the clarity, discernment, and judgment to consistently make great decisions.
